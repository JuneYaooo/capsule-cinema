# Structure

## Rules

- Default to the local short silent route: 4-5 pages, 默认成片固定 15 秒, no narration track, no burned subtitles. The canvas is fixed at 1080×1260 (6:7); the active title, source, fact-card, and footer layout stays between y=30 and y=1230. The warm-white/orange-grid background remains continuous across the full canvas with no blank top or bottom bands. The 6:7 route reduces vertical height and reflows the three content zones instead of scaling or stretching the 9:16 frame. Batch/profile generation must not shorten or lengthen the approved render.
- Before arranging pages, complete the repo topic scorecard, select one primary topic formula, and identify the target user, specific task, hard detail, evidence source, forward receiver, open judgment, and risk boundary. A weak topic must be narrowed or skipped instead of being stretched into 4-5 pages.
- Build the whole 4-5-page arc around `用户判断或任务身份 -> 项目动作与硬证据 -> 使用结果、边界或未闭合问题`. Each page advances one major judgment while retaining the existing 4-5-line complete fact-chain card contract.
- Each page should add one useful reason to keep watching. Choose the page order by the strongest one-glance value: result, hidden resource, old-way contrast, risk warning, proof number, demo shock, mechanism, or practical next step.
- Before accepting a page, bind its bottom fact-chain judgment to one named proof region in the middle visual. If the screenshot cannot visibly prove or contextualize that judgment at phone size, tighten the capture, change the fact, or drop the page.
- Default subject count is one repo, one Skill, or one tool. If a video truly covers multiple Skills/modules/tools, reserve a small dedicated subject_paths area for 3-5 representative short paths, and keep the main title about the shared office task or result.
- The top badge identifies the exact object (`owner/repo` or a short Skill path); the title explains what the object helps the viewer do and why it is worth attention.
- The fixed top title is a two-line large title: line 1 is the viewer-value hook, line 2 is the exact Skill/Skills type. Keep type identity in the large title itself, not only in `top_subtitle`, so a phone preview can read both "what value" and "what skills/tool category" without hunting.
- The bottom fact card is one visible 4-5 行 chain, not a separate headline plus filler. `bottom_title` must be empty or omitted; if the renderer receives a non-empty `bottom_title`, preflight fails. Each visible line should read like one natural viewer decision and may combine scene/problem, special mechanism, evidence, result, boundary, or tradeoff in the order that makes the value most obvious. Keep each line complete enough to read naturally, and short enough to stay readable in a phone preview; longer lines are allowed when the contact sheet proves they fit.
- The last page is not a platform CTA. Use it for a concrete handoff such as what to ask the agent, what material to prepare, what output to expect, or where the project stops being suitable.
- Do not stretch a repo into a generic product tour. The structure must make the target viewer understand whether this project is worth trying, saving, or forwarding.
