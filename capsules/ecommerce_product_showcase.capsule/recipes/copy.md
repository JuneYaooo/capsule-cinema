# Copy

## copy_rules

- Use one selling point per scene; do not stack more than three public selling points in a 20-second script.
- Do not invent prices, discounts, medical effects, safety certifications, platform badges, review counts, delivery promises, or absolute claims.
- If price_or_promo is empty, CTA should be value-led or soft-guidance only, such as “点开看看细节” or “先收藏这个小工具”.
- Avoid manipulative urgency unless the user explicitly provided a real limited-time promotion.
- For regulated categories such as health, beauty, finance, children, food safety, and devices, keep claims descriptive and add uncertainty-safe phrasing.
