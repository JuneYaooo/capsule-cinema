# Visual

## style_rules

- First visual impression must be strong ink on paper: black-gray layers, ivory paper, dry-brush edges, and wet ink spreading.
- Use muted red, brown, and warm gold only as restrained accents; avoid neon, glossy fantasy color, and high-saturation game art.
- Scene composition should include negative space and layered depth; do not fill every frame with detail.
- Reject weak guofeng backgrounds where the subject could be any generic ancient figure without narrative identity.
