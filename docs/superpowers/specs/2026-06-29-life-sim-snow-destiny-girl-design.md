# Life Sim Snow Destiny Girl Video Design

Date: 2026-06-29

## Goal

Produce a horizontal `life_sim` capsule video inspired by the public image of Gu Ailing/Eileen Gu, with a high-impact anime sports-drama style and pure "爽文" pacing. The video should feel like an exaggerated life-simulation episode rather than a factual documentary or impersonation.

Working title:

> 每天一个模拟人生：今天抽到雪场天命少女

The viewer experience should be: born with an impossible snow-sports destiny, trained harder than everyone else, hit pressure and public doubt, then choose the hardest final jump and land it in a visually explosive climax.

## User Locks

- Capsule: `life_sim`.
- Aspect ratio: horizontal `16:9`, using the capsule default.
- Runtime: under 5 minutes, target 4:00-4:30.
- Story shape: from birth to a major competition climax.
- Tone: pure high-energy "爽文", not reflective documentary.
- Visual style: attractive, cinematic anime, strong impact, sports-anime energy.
- Subject framing: Gu Ailing/Eileen Gu-inspired, but not a deepfake, not a voice clone, not a simulated real statement from her.

## Public-Fact Anchors

Use public facts only as light inspiration, not as a strict biography:

- FIS athlete information gives Ailing Eileen Gu's birthdate as 2003-09-03.
- Red Bull's athlete profile lists date of birth as 3 September 2003 and place of birth as San Francisco, USA.
- Olympics.com identifies Gu Ailing Eileen as an Olympic champion freestyle skier.
- NBC Olympics public profile material describes early skiing from age three and junior success by age nine; these details can inform the "early destiny" arc.

Reference URLs to keep with internal notes, not as viewer-facing text:

- `https://www.fis-ski.com/DB/general/athlete-biography.html?sectorcode=FS&competitorid=215140&type=result`
- `https://www.redbull.com/int-en/athlete/eileen-gu`
- `https://www.olympics.com/en/athletes/gu-ailing-eileen`
- `https://www.nbcolympics.com/athletes/eileen-gu-1114358`

These anchors should not turn the video into a fact-by-fact biography. The finished piece should be labeled and structured as a fictionalized life-simulation episode inspired by a famous freeski archetype.

## Safety And Rights Boundaries

- Do not recreate Gu Ailing's exact face, voice, signature, endorsements, real interviews, or private speech.
- Do not imply she approved, narrated, sponsored, or appeared in the video.
- Do not use real Olympic, FIS, brand, sponsor, school, platform, or team logos.
- Do not ask image generation to render Chinese text, UI text, medals text, scoreboards, or news comments.
- Do not create fabricated factual allegations or private-life claims.
- If the platform copy mentions her, it should say "谷爱凌式" or "受谷爱凌式雪场爽文人生启发", not "谷爱凌本人".
- Viewer-facing on-screen text should use the fictional identity: "雪场天命少女".

## Viewer Pull

Primary audience:

- Viewers who like sports-anime, 爽文, and "人生副本" narratives.
- Viewers who recognize the Gu Ailing archetype: genius, snow, big-air pressure, public attention.
- Short-drama viewers who want fast escalation, visual spectacle, and a final-jump payoff.

Stop reason in first 3 seconds:

- The life-sim shaker opens with "雪场天命少女".
- The first body shot immediately shows a baby-room destiny card and snow light, not a slow biographical setup.

Takeaway:

- Viewers remember one sentence: "她不是不会摔，是每次摔完都把雪地当成下一页。"

Wrong audience:

- Viewers expecting a factual documentary, real Gu Ailing footage, exact competition reconstruction, or real-person impersonation.

## Narrative Design

Use second-person narration, matching successful `life_sim` long-form runs: "你出生..." "你第一次站上雪板..." "最后一跳，你没有选稳。"

The structure should have 10 major beats:

1. **出生异象**  
   A sea-city baby room, cold blue daylight, a fictional destiny panel, snow drifting where there should be no snow. Hook line: "你出生那天，系统没有发哭声提示，它只弹出一行字：雪场适配度 99%。"

2. **三岁上雪**  
   A small child on skis, too tiny for the slope, falling and laughing. The slope is framed like the first boss map.

3. **童年训练**  
   Weekend car windows, pre-dawn roads, empty snowfields, frozen gloves, repeated falls. The narration reframes talent as repetition: "别人说你有天赋，是因为他们没看见你把同一个摔倒练了一百遍。"

4. **九岁通关初级地图**  
   A junior contest, small podium, a larger mountain in the background. The trophy matters less than the next map opening.

5. **双身份与全服质疑**  
   Abstract camera flashes, split-light city/snow imagery, floating pressure tags added in post only if needed. No real comments, no readable generated UI.

6. **难度条拉满**  
   Big air, halfpipe, slopestyle-like visual language without official event marks. The visual rhythm becomes more manga-like and kinetic.

7. **摔倒重置**  
   A hard fall in training, snow exploding, the world muffled. No gore. The energy drops for contrast, then rebuilds.

8. **赛前夜**  
   A lonely room, skis leaning beside the bed, abstract screens glowing, outside voices compressed into noise. This is the calm before the boss fight.

9. **最终一跳**  
   The climax should last 30-45 seconds. She chooses the hardest line, accelerates, launches, rotates through a white-sky void, lands, and the snowfield flashes open like a completed map.

10. **系统结算**  
    No moralizing. End with a direct爽文 settlement: "模拟结束。雪场天命少女，通关。"

## Visual System

Character anchor:

- Fictional young Chinese/Chinese-American freeski heroine.
- Athletic, sharp-eyed, expressive, long dark hair tied high or tucked into helmet.
- White and silver snow suit with small red accents, red goggles, no logos.
- The face should be consistent enough to read as the same character, but not a copy of Gu Ailing.

Anime style:

- High-end sports anime film frame.
- Strong foreground/midground/background composition.
- Snow glare, speed trails, powder bursts, rim light, cold blue shadows, warm gold climax light.
- Clean hand-drawn feature-animation texture with cinematic depth.
- Not chibi, not cute mascot, not flat webtoon, not photoreal celebrity imitation.

Impact rules:

- Every 1-3 second micro-cut must introduce a new state: new age, new location, new posture, new pressure, new action, or new emotional turn.
- Use low-angle shots, over-the-shoulder takeoff views, extreme close-ups of boots/goggles/hands, wide mountain silhouettes, and aerial rotation frames.
- The final-jump sequence should feel bigger than the rest: sky-whitened negative space, slowed time, snow particles suspended, then hard landing impact.
- Avoid static "person standing in snow" shots except as deliberate calm-before-impact beats.

Color arc:

- Birth/childhood: pale blue and warm nursery gold.
- Training: cold blue, dawn violet, fluorescent snowfield lights.
- Pressure: split red/blue, compressed shadows, camera-flash white.
- Climax: white, electric cyan, then gold sunrise.

## Audio And Text

Narration:

- Chinese second-person voiceover.
- Use `life_sim` default energetic narration approach, preferably MiniMax male narrator if available through the capsule config; do not clone Gu Ailing or use a female celebrity-like voice.
- TTS duration is the timing authority.

BGM:

- Low-volume cinematic sports-anime bed under narration.
- Build from restrained pulse to high-energy final jump.
- Keep narration intelligible; target BGM volume in the `life_sim` safe range.

Visible text:

- Opening shaker text and top scene labels only.
- No bottom body narration subtitles unless separately requested.
- On-screen text examples: "出生异象", "三岁上雪", "初级地图通关", "全服质疑", "最终一跳", "系统结算".
- No viewer-facing production labels such as draft, v1, revision, source, real asset, or storyboard.

## Production Architecture

The production uses the `life_sim` capsule as the governing contract:

1. Inspect `life_sim` contract and active channel policy.
2. Create a storyboard package with major beats, second-person narration, micro-cut timeline, and one Image2 prompt per body micro-cut.
3. Run the `life_sim` local-script dry-run with merged params to confirm contract checks and image budget.
4. Generate a strong representative prototype frame for the final-jump climax before batching the full image set.
5. Generate all unique body keyframes with approved image tooling, preserving the character/style anchor.
6. Render the shaker opening with the existing `life_shaker_opening_renderer.py`.
7. Generate TTS, measure it with `ffprobe`, and let measured audio drive final scene durations.
8. Assemble clean visual base, BGM, SFX, top labels, and public copy into a versioned release package.
9. Run QA and compliance before claiming the video is publishable.

The current `life_sim_executor.py` blocks full render unless a storyboard package with per-micro-cut prompts exists. This project therefore treats the storyboard package as a required artifact before any paid batch generation. If the executor still lacks the concrete render backend after the package exists, implementation should extend or wrap the local-script route in a way that preserves the capsule gates, rather than bypassing the capsule rules silently.

## Generation Budget

Target runtime is 4:00-4:30. After a 3.5-4.5 second opening, the body will be roughly 235-265 seconds.

At `life_sim` micro-cut density, expected body images:

- Conservative 2.4 seconds per cut: about 98-110 unique images.
- Recommended 2.0 seconds per cut: about 118-133 unique images.
- Maximum-impact 1.6 seconds per cut: about 147-166 unique images.

Use the recommended range unless the prototype shows the final jump needs denser cutting. The user has explicitly asked for strong visual impact, so the final-jump section can use denser 1.2-1.8 second cuts while calmer setup sections use 2.2-2.8 second cuts.

## Error Handling

- If the image tool produces celebrity-like facial resemblance, regenerate with a stronger fictional-character anchor.
- If final-jump prototype lacks impact, do not batch; revise style and composition first.
- If TTS fails, stop rather than silently switching to an unapproved voice or local fallback.
- If BGM covers narration, reduce volume and re-mix before final QA.
- If any micro-cut reuses an image path or content hash, regenerate the duplicate shots.
- If visible text lint catches internal production terms, rewrite public copy and rerun lint.
- If compliance flags deepfake/impersonation risk, soften naming and reinforce fictional framing before delivery.

## Testing And QA

Required checks before delivery:

- `life_sim` dry-run contract report passes blocker checks.
- `ffprobe` confirms final duration, audio track, and `16:9` dimensions.
- Local decode check confirms no black frames or broken video.
- `local_video_qa.py` passes for aspect ratio and audio presence.
- `visible_copy_lint.py` passes on viewer-facing text only.
- `qa/micro_cut_report.json` proves unique image paths and unique content hashes for body micro-cuts.
- Manual alignment audit confirms narration, scene labels, opening text, and images tell the same story.
- Self-media compliance review reports no unresolved Blocker risks and no unaccepted High risks.
- Release manifest points to the current public video, cover/copy if present, QA report, compliance report, and clean concat base.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A complete horizontal anime-style life-sim video under 5 minutes exists in the release package.
- The first 5 seconds clearly show the `life_sim` shaker identity and the "snow destiny girl" hook.
- The story starts at birth and escalates through childhood, training, doubt, injury/reset, pre-competition pressure, and final-jump climax.
- The climax is visually stronger than the setup and contains the highest-density cutting.
- The main character is compelling and consistent but not a photoreal imitation of Gu Ailing.
- No real logos, real UI, generated text, or body subtitles are burned into image-model outputs.
- The final artifact passes technical, visible-copy, micro-cut, and compliance QA gates.
